This WASP-HS research environment brings together scholars from media technology, gender studies, and the history of science to investigate the societal dimensions of synthetic data — computer-generated representations increasingly used across research, healthcare, and industry. Combining critical algorithm studies, computational experiments, and intersectional analysis, the group develops vocabularies, methods, and infrastructure to ensure that synthetic data is transparent, reproducible, and ethically integrated.

The environment is led by Francis Lee (Södertörn University), Ericka Johnson (Linköping University), and Ylva Söderfeldt (Uppsala University), and is funded by the Wallenberg AI, Autonomous Systems and Software Program – Humanities and Society (WASP-HS).
