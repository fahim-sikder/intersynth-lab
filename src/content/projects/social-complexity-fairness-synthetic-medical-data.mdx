Funded through the WASP-HS Networking Excellence (NetX) program, this project brought together social scientists and computer scientists to investigate fairness in synthetic medical data. The team examined how machine learning pipelines tend to over-represent "standard" patients while marginalizing minorities, and applied intersectional frameworks to develop metrics that expose and address these disparities. The collaboration was structured around a two-year postdoc position bridging WASP-HS and WASP research communities.
