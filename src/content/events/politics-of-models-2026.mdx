This workshop, co-organized by WASP and WASP-HS, brings together researchers to examine how generative AI models function as new forms of knowledge representation—and how technical choices such as applied ontologies and categorizations influence power, culture, and history.

Topics include representation and bias across modalities, transparency and accountability frameworks, and the societal alignment of technical solutions.

The workshop is organized by Kıvanç Tatar (Chalmers University of Technology), Kathlén Kohn (KTH Royal Institute of Technology), Md Fahim Sikder (Linköping University), Francis Lee (Södertörn University), and Bodil Formark (Södertörn University).
