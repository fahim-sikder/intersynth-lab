The Swedish STS Conference 2026 brings together researchers from across Sweden and beyond to explore science and technology studies from social science and humanities perspectives. Held at the Niagara building at Malmö University, the conference theme "Cross-Pollinations, Contamination, Collaboration" addresses how boundary-crossing collaborations can foster innovation and societal change in the face of pressing global challenges—including climate change, artificial intelligence, warfare, infectious diseases, and migration.

The conference is open to researchers at all career levels and includes dedicated sessions for doctoral students and early-career researchers.

Several members of the Intersynth Lab will be attending and presenting.
