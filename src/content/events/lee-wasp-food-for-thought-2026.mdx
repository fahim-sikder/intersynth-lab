Francis Lee spoke at Food for Thought #2: Self-evolving AI, a WASP and WASP-HS lunch webinar moderated by Fredrik Heintz, together with Jennifer Horkoff (Chalmers) and Stefan Larsson (Lund University).

The talk asked who decides what counts as improvement when AI systems train themselves. Drawing on STS and valuation studies, it argued that every self-evolving system depends on a yardstick – a test, a score or a benchmark – that someone has chosen. What is “good” is rarely settled, especially for language, art and culture, so choosing the score is a political choice. Self-evolving AI does not remove people from the loop. It hides which people are in it.
