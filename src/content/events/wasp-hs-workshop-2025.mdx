A one-day workshop organized by Ericka Johnson, Francis Lee, and Ylva Söderfeldt, supported by the WASP-HS Wallenberg AI, Autonomous Systems and Software Program.

The workshop brought together over 15 participants from diverse disciplines — humanities, social sciences, ML, open science data repositories, and libraries — to collaboratively examine what synthetic data is, how it is evaluated, and how it should be shared responsibly.

Working in groups, participants produced *zines* addressing four themes:

- **Proxies** — synthetic data as a stand-in for reality
- **Relationship dynamics** — how synthetic and real data interact
- **Quality assessment** — confidence scores, performance metrics, and manual verification
- **Responsible sharing** — synthetic data in open science contexts
