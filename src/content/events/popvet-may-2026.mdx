Francis Lee presents at Södertörns högskolas PopVet lecture series, exploring how AI-generated datasets are reshaping modern research. The talk examines both opportunities and risks when simulated models replace real observations, and challenges our understanding of what research actually builds upon.

**When:** 12:15–13:00
**Where:** Bibliotekets lounge, ground floor, Alfred Nobels allé 7, Flemingsberg

Free admission, no registration required. The first 30 attendees receive a free lunch sandwich.
