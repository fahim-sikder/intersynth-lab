A workshop held at Aarhus University (Building 5335, Room 184) on August 18, 2025, also available online as part of the Aarhus 2025 Conference.

Organized by Hannah Devinney, Katherine Harrison, Vagrant Gautam, Irina Shklovski, and Eddie Ungless, the workshop explored how synthetic data addresses data scarcity and privacy concerns while raising questions about what "representativeness" actually means — distinguishing between quantitative dataset composition metrics and the qualities assigned to groups in training data.
