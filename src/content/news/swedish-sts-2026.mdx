Several members of the Intersynth Lab will be attending the Swedish STS Conference 2026, held June 10–12 at the Niagara building at Malmö University.

The conference theme — "Cross-Pollinations, Contamination, Collaboration" — brings together researchers in science and technology studies to examine how boundary-crossing collaboration can foster innovation and societal change. The program addresses pressing global challenges including climate change, artificial intelligence, warfare, infectious diseases, and migration, from social science and humanities perspectives.

The conference is organized by Malmö University and Lund University and is open to researchers at all career levels.
