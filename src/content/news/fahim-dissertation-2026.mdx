Md Fahim Sikder has successfully defended his doctoral dissertation *Representative Synthetic Data for Fair Decision Making* at Linköping University.

The dissertation develops generative models and fair representation learning techniques that address intersectional bias in AI decision-making systems. The work spans fairness-aware synthetic health data generation, diffusion-based time series synthesis, and knowledge distillation approaches for producing fair synthetic datasets.
