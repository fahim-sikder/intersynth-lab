Alicja Ostrowska has successfully defended her doctoral dissertation *Life and AI at NASA: An Ethnography of How Scientists and Engineers Make Tools to Explore Other Worlds* at Chalmers University of Technology.

Based on fieldwork at NASA Goddard Space Flight Center, the dissertation investigates how AI is reshaping knowledge production in planetary science — and how the data used to train these tools often reflects the prestige and accessibility of Earth locations rather than their relevance to other worlds.
